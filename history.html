Bria & Maurice

Through The Years

Every great love story has a beginning, and theirs started long before they knew what the future would hold. As childhood sweethearts, Bria and Maurice grew up together, sharing dreams, milestones, and memories that would shape the lives they were building side by side. Through graduations, the joys of parenthood, becoming homeowners, and finally saying "I Do," each chapter brought them closer together. What began as young love grew into a lasting partnership founded on friendship, trust, and unwavering commitment. Years have passed, families have grown, and dreams have become reality, but one thing has remained the same: they have always chosen each other. This is the story of Bria and Maurice—a journey from childhood sweethearts to forever.
